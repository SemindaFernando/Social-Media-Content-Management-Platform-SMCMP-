2. Hotel Data Protection & GDPR Statement

Grand Azure Resort & Spa adheres strictly to global data protection regulations, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  • Right to Privacy: No guest likeness, family photos, or identifiable children are published on social media without explicit, signed digital waivers.
  • Data Minimization: Analytics tracked via SMCMP aggregate anonymous reach metrics only; no PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is transmitted to third-party ad networks without consent.
  • Right to Erasure (Takedown Policy): Any guest wishing to revoke consent may request immediate post removal via privacy@grandazurehotel.com (processed within 24 hours).
3. Hotel Social Media Brand Voice & Staff Guidelines
  • Brand Tone & Voice: Always maintain a warm, refined, five-star tone. Avoid slang, defensive arguments, or low-resolution imagery.
  • Guest Confidentiality: Hotel staff and content creators must never disclose VIP guest names, room numbers, or itinerary details on social channels.
  • Crisis Escalation Protocol: For negative viral mentions, service complaints, or emergency incidents, immediately escalate to Elena Rostova (Content Approver) and GM Victoria Sterling. Do not reply publicly without PR approval.
  • Copyright & Music Licensing: All background audio used in TikToks and Reels must be licensed through Grand Azure’s commercial BMI / ASCAP hotel master licensing agreement.
